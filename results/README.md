# Experimental Results and Characterization

This directory contains experimental measurements and analysis performed during the development of the CGM prototype.

## Measurements

- ADC raw data acquisition.
- TIA output voltage measurements.
- Sensor current calculation.
- Electrical response characterization.
- Signal acquisition and stability observations.

## Sample Measurements

## Results and Analysis

Experimental data and corresponding visualization plots are organized into separate directories.

- `raw_data/` – Measurement datasets.
- `plots/` – Graphs and visualization of experimental measurements.

## Limitations

These measurements demonstrate prototype-level electrical characterization. They do not establish clinically accurate glucose measurements or a validated glucose calibration model.
